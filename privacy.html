Proview Jobs privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Proview Jobs is the job management app used by Proview Tech to create jobs, assign them to staff and track them to completion. This page explains what information the app holds and what happens to it.

Who is responsible

Proview Tech runs this app and decides how the information in it is used. To ask a question or make a request about your information, write to Proview Tech by post at its business address, or speak to your Proview Tech manager.

What the app holds

  • Team members: name, sign-in email, role (manager or staff) and, where given, a WhatsApp number.
  • Jobs: what the job is, the customer's name, phone number and address where a manager has entered them, the due date, tasks, parts, completion notes and photos taken on the job.
  • WhatsApp messages: messages sent to the Proview Jobs number by the team's approved numbers, which the app turns into jobs. These are kept so a manager can see what was received and which job it created. Messages from any other number are not stored.
  • Sign-in details: your sign-in email and a protected (hashed) copy of your password.

What it is used for

Only to run the work: creating and assigning jobs, letting staff see and complete the jobs given to them, and sending job notices by WhatsApp to approved numbers. The information is not sold, not shared for advertising and not used to build profiles of anyone.

Who can see it

  • Managers can see all jobs and the team list.
  • Staff can see only the jobs assigned to them.
  • Nobody outside the team has access.

Where it is kept

The information is stored with Supabase in a data centre in London, United Kingdom. WhatsApp messages are sent and received through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which handles them under Meta's own terms.

How long it is kept

Jobs, photos and message records are kept until a manager deletes them. A team member's details are kept while they work with Proview Tech and removed when a manager removes them from the team.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information held about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, by writing to Proview Tech. If you are in the UK you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
